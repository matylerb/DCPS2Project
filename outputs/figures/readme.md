# Visualisations

## 01 — Cancellations by Day of Week
Bar chart showing total cancelled Dublin Bus trips per weekday.
Monday and Tuesday have significantly higher cancellations (8,099 and 10,456),
while Wednesday–Friday are moderate (~2,359 each). No cancellations on weekends.

## 02 — Cancellations Over Time
Line chart showing how cancellations evolved across the dataset period (April–September 2026).
Peaks are visible in early April (10,456) and again in May–June (8,099),
with a notable drop-off from July onwards.

## 03 — Heatmap: Top 20 Routes by Day of Week
Heatmap showing cancellation patterns for the 20 most affected routes across weekdays.
Routes like E1, E2, 15, and 39A are concentrated on Monday–Tuesday,
while S4, N6, and W2 show consistent cancellations Tuesday–Friday.

## 04 — Top 20 Most Cancelled Routes
Horizontal bar chart ranking the 20 routes with the most total cancellations.
Route S4 leads with 856 cancelled trips, followed by N6 (720) and 73 (696).
